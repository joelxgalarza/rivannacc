Our Story

Why Rivanna Home Cleaning Exists

Rivanna Home Cleaning was started to give Charlottesville homeowners, renters, landlords, and small businesses a cleaning service they can actually count on: someone who shows up when promised, communicates clearly, and does the work well without being asked twice.

We take our name from the Rivanna River that runs through the area we call home. As a new, locally owned company, we're building our reputation one clean at a time, which is exactly why we back every visit with our 100% Satisfaction Guarantee.
