How We Do It

A deep clean works top to bottom through every room you include. We start high with light fixtures, vent covers, and ceiling fan blades, and we work down through window sills, door frames, and baseboards. Tile and grout get a real scrub instead of a pass with a mop. Kitchen work covers appliance exteriors, cabinet fronts, and the grease that collects around the range. Bathrooms get descaled fixtures, scrubbed tile, and the corners behind the toilet that routine visits skip. Floors get vacuumed and mopped last so nothing resettles on a clean surface.

You set the scope when you book. Inside the oven and inside the fridge come with the visit when you request them. Clutter slows detail work, so clearing counters and floors before we arrive gets you the most from the appointment. We bring our own supplies and equipment, and you do not need to provide a thing. A deep clean also works well as a first visit before recurring maid service, since the detail pass sets a baseline that weekly, biweekly, or monthly visits can hold.

Around Charlottesville

What Makes It Different Here

Older homes near walkable downtown districts carry detailed trim, tall baseboards, and original windows with tracks that trap dust. That woodwork looks beautiful, and it asks for hand wiping instead of a quick dusting pass. Open-plan new builds and production-builder subdivisions bring a different job with large kitchens, tall windows, and glass shower doors that show every water spot.

The calendar shapes demand through the year. Spring pollen season coats sills and floors, so spring runs as deep-clean season and the schedule fills early. Lease turnover peaks in late spring and late summer bring move-related work, and hospital and shift-worker scheduling calls for flexible visit windows. Booking ahead of those stretches matters even more.
