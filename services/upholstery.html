How We Do It

Every piece starts with a fabric check, because the wrong solution on the wrong material does real harm. Then cushions and crevices get vacuumed, visible spots treated, and the piece cleaned with solutions matched to the fabric. Odor treatment is available for pieces that need it.

Drying stays low-moisture, which protects padding and frames and gets the furniture back in use sooner. List your pieces, and their fabrics if you know them, on the quote form, and point out the worst stains when we arrive.

Living Rooms Around Charlottesville

What Makes It Different Here

Pets shape this job more than any other service. Dogs claim the same couch corner in every neighborhood in town, and dander weaves into fabric deeper than a lint roller reaches. Spring is the busy season: pollen rides in on fur and settles straight into soft surfaces.

Rental turnover brings the other steady stream. Furnished rentals absorb a full lease of use into every shared sofa, and turnover season runs late spring into late summer. A turnover clean that includes the furniture hands back a living room that looks rented, not worn out.
