Built Around Your Workday

How We Do It

Each visit covers desks and common areas, the kitchen and break room, glass and reception, plus trash and recycling. Desks get cleaned around your work, not through it: papers, equipment, and personal items stay put. Your checklist is confirmed at booking, so every visit covers the same scope.

After-hours and early-morning slots keep the work invisible to your team. Keys, codes, and alarm notes go on the quote form, and the same crew returns when possible. Frequency runs nightly to monthly, set by headcount and foot traffic.

Offices Around Charlottesville

What Makes It Different Here

Small professional offices set the shape of this work: downtown firms, corridor suites, practices near the hospitals. The job is consistency more than muscle: a crew that shows up reliably, works quietly, and locks up right. A missed week shows faster in a ten-person office than in a tower.

Break rooms carry more weight than their square footage suggests. Small shared kitchens turn grimy fast, and every office has its own rules about dishes and restocking. Tell us how your team actually uses the space and the checklist gets built around it.
