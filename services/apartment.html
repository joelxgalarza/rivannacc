One-Time or Recurring, Sized to Your Unit

How We Do It

An apartment clean covers the full unit in one visit. Kitchen and bathrooms get the full reset. Living areas and bedrooms get dusted, vacuumed, and mopped, and trash goes out. The quote reflects your unit and layout instead of a routine built for a house.

Put your building setup on the quote form and we work around it. Gate codes, garage parking, elevator rules, and leasing-office check-ins are all normal to us. Tell us the unit size and layout, and whether you want a one-time visit or a recurring plan. We bring our own supplies and equipment either way.

Lease Calendars and Tight Kitchens

What Makes It Different Here

Apartment life runs on the lease calendar. Student rentals empty out in late spring and fill back up in late summer, and those turnover peaks bring a wave of end-of-lease cleans. Large complexes with working renters keep a steadier rhythm on recurring plans, while condos in walkable downtown districts mix owners and long-term tenants who book one-time resets and seasonal deep work.

Smaller kitchens and shared laundry areas shape the job too. Apartment kitchens collect grease fast in tight quarters, and bathrooms with weak ventilation need real descaling attention. We size the checklist to the unit: the rooms you have, not a routine built for a four-bedroom house.
