Top to Bottom, in Passes

How We Do It

Construction dust gets everywhere, so we work top to bottom in passes. High surfaces, vents, and fixtures first. Then windows, cabinet fronts, and every flat surface where dust resettles. Floors come last. Light debris haul-out can be added when you book: list what the crew left behind on the quote form.

Book after the final trade finishes, punch-list touchups included, or fresh dust undoes the work. If you have curing paint or newly refinished floors, mention it and we plan products and pressure around them. We bring our own supplies and equipment to every visit.

Building Around Charlottesville

What Makes It Different Here

New builds in the subdivisions keep this service busy. Buyers close on finished homes that still need a full dust-out before furniture arrives, and open floor plans carry fine dust a long way. Those jobs pair naturally with a move-in clean once the dust is gone.

Renovation work downtown looks different. Older homes open up plaster that sheds dust for weeks, and tall baseboards and original trim want hand wiping, not aggressive scrubbing. New build or gutted kitchen, describe the project on the quote form and the phone quote reflects the actual scope.
