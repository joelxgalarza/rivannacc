How We Do It

We clean the space empty, and timing does the heavy lifting. Book us after the last box leaves for a move-out, or before the first box arrives for a move-in. Empty rooms let us reach every corner, and the visit runs faster without furniture in the way. Tell us your truck window and key handoff time on the quote form, and we plan the visit around them.

Inside the home, the work runs room by room. Cabinets, drawers, and closets get wiped inside and out. The kitchen gets a deep reset with appliance exteriors, cabinet fronts, and scrubbed sinks and counters. Bathrooms get descaled fixtures, scrubbed tile, and detailed floors. Baseboards get wiped through the whole home, and floors get vacuumed and mopped last. We bring our own supplies and equipment, so you do not need to leave a single sponge behind.

Around Charlottesville

What Makes It Different Here

Rental turnovers follow the lease calendar. Leases peak in late spring and late summer, and those weeks fill the schedule with apartment and house turnovers. Property managers walk units with a checklist, and inside cabinets plus wiped baseboards show up on nearly every one. A move-out clean that covers those details by default gives you a stronger handoff.

Older rentals carry detailed trim and tall baseboards that collect a year of dust in tracks and corners. Newer apartments and townhomes in production-builder subdivisions turn faster with simpler lines and bigger kitchens. Either way, book a few days out for the best slot, and book earlier around lease turnover peaks when every cleaner runs full.
