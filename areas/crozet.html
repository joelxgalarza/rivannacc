How we work here

Construction dust follows new Crozet homes indoors

Crozet blends a historic small-town core along its walkable Main Street with fast-growing subdivisions like Old Trail spreading toward the Blue Ridge foothills. Many of our Crozet visits are move-in cleans for freshly built homes, plus recurring service for families on the established streets.

Active building zones leave fine dust on everything nearby, and new homes carry residue from drywall, paint, and flooring work in vents, sills, and closet shelves. Our post-construction and move-in cleans wipe every surface top to bottom, detail inside cabinets and drawers, and vacuum tracks, vents, and fixtures.

Freshly finished homes need care with new surfaces: quartz and granite counters, luxury vinyl and new hardwood, glass shower enclosures. We match products to each finish so new kitchens and baths start clean and stay that way.

Out in the county, well water and red clay add their own marks, from mineral spots on fixtures to muddy paw prints across entry floors. We treat water spots on glass and chrome, and damp mop entries and mudrooms so clay never grinds into new floors.
