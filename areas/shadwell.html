How we work here

Wooded lots and large floor plans make dust control the main job

Shadwell is a quiet community east of Charlottesville along Route 250, where farmhouses, brick colonials, and newer homes sit on generous lots with trees and open lawns.

Mature trees drop spring pollen on every porch rail, screen, and windowsill. We wipe sills and tracks, mop entry floors where pollen gets tracked in, and clean interior glass so the film does not build up through the season.

Larger floor plans mean more baseboards, more ceiling fans, and more corners where dust gathers untouched. We clean room by room from the ceiling line down, hitting fans, vents, blinds, and trim before floors, so each room stays clean once we leave it.

Dogs are part of life on larger lots, and their hair works into carpet edges, stair corners, and sofa cushions. We vacuum upholstery and stairs with crevice tools, mop hard floors to lift dander, and wipe the nose prints off patio doors and low windows.
