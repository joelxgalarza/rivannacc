House Cleaning in Free Union, VA

Free Union is a tiny hamlet in northwestern Albemarle County, a crossroads of a country school, a post office, and several dozen homes surrounded by farms and estates.

Properties here run large, with acreage homes, horse farms, and vineyard land set against Blue Ridge views and deep quiet.

How we work here

Big rural homes collect dust faster than owners can chase it

Field dust and tree pollen drift in through windows and doors for much of the year. We start high, wiping sills, vents, and fan blades before floors, so dust leaves the house instead of resettling.

Walls of windows frame mountain views but show every speck. We detail glass, sills, and tracks, then finish the floors beneath them, so bright rooms look crisp from every angle.

Large homes with guest wings and gathering spaces fill up when family comes to town. A deep clean ahead of company, followed by regular visits, keeps the whole property ready.
