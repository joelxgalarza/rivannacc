How we work here

Busy family homes stay cleanest with steady upkeep

Route 29 traffic and open parking lots push a steady film of dust toward nearby homes. We wipe sills, shelves, and vents every visit and finish floors last, so entry dust never gets dragged back through clean rooms.

Kitchens, family rooms, and the stairs between them take the daily beating in two-story homes and townhouses. We give those zones full attention each visit, vacuum pet fur from rugs and sofa edges, and rotate baseboards and fans so detail work never piles up.

Hollymead specifics

Easy scheduling around workdays and school days

Hollymead households often run on two work schedules plus school pickup, and you do not need to be home for the visit. Put entry instructions in the quote form and we will confirm the plan when we call.

Townhouse parking and shared walkways need a little thought on visit day. We park where your community asks and carry supplies in quietly, keeping stairs and entries clear while we work.
