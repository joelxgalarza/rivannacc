How we work here

New construction dust lingers long after move-in day

Zion Crossroads straddles the Fluvanna and Louisa county line where Interstate 64 meets Route 15, and new subdivisions and apartments have followed the shops and offices, with open floor plans, big kitchens, and builder-grade finishes.

New homes shed fine construction dust for months after closing, and it hides in vent covers, blinds, ceiling fans, and closet shelves. We dust every one of those surfaces on a deep clean, then vacuum and mop floors so the dust leaves for good.

Builder-grade kitchens and baths show water spots and streaks fast on new faucets, glass doors, and stainless steel. We wipe fixtures with cleaners suited to each finish, polish glass until spots are gone, and scrub grout lines before they stain.

Open floor plans let kitchen grease and daily dust travel far across hard floors and area rugs. We mop the full open space edge to edge, vacuum rugs and runners, and wipe the baseboards where dust lines collect along the walls.
