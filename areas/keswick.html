How we work here

Estate homes and farmhouses each need their own cleaning approach

Open fields and gravel drives send dust, pollen, and fine grit through every opened door. We treat entries and mudrooms as the first line of defense, then work through living areas top to bottom so airborne dust leaves with us.

Wide-plank floors, stone fireplaces, tall windows, and formal dining rooms reward patience, while dogs, boots, and hard-working laundry rooms take the beating. We match cloths and pads to each surface, spend extra time on sills, mantels, and glass, and rotate guest suites and offices across visits.

Keswick specifics
