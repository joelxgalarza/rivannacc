How we work here

New construction dust hides in every vent and sill

Rivanna Village is a newer community beside Glenmore in eastern Albemarle, built out with villas, townhouses, and single-family homes. Streets, finishes, and floor plans all read fresh, and many households arrive here straight from a recent closing.

New builds shed fine construction dust for months after handover, coating vents, blinds, sills, and closet shelves. We detail those surfaces first, then finish floors, so the dust leaves the home for good.

Fresh paint, new grout, and new appliances need a gentle approach. We use soft cloths and neutral products on new finishes and give grout lines and glass a patient first pass.

Move timing often stacks the clean against furniture delivery and box unpacking. We sequence the visit room by room, starting with bedrooms and baths, so the home is livable while boxes remain.
