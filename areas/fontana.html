How we work here

Busy family schedules let Fontana homes fall behind fast

River-valley pollen settles on everything in spring, and trail dust follows kids and dogs home from Darden Towe. We work top to bottom each visit, clearing sills and vents before floors.

Two-story colonials with finished basements spread living space across three levels. We rotate detail areas like baseboards, ceiling fans, and stair railings, so every level stays covered across visits.

Kitchens take the heaviest use in family homes. We degrease stovetops and range hoods, wipe cabinet fronts, and detail sinks, so the hardest-working room stays presentable.
