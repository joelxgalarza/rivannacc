How we work here

River dust, pollen, and old house grime need extra attention

Milton is a quiet riverside community in eastern Albemarle County, set along the Rivanna below Monticello mountain. Historic farmhouses from the old river-port days sit on generous lots beside newer houses tucked into the fields and woods.

Homes along the Rivanna corridor pick up fine dust and spring pollen that settles on sills, porch rails, and floors near every entry. We work top to bottom in these rooms, clearing vents, sills, and baseboards before floors, so dust does not resettle on finished surfaces.

Milton farmhouses often have plaster walls, wide plank floors, and original trim that need a gentle hand and the right cloth. We dry dust delicate woodwork, vacuum crevices with soft brushes, and treat worn floors with products suited to older finishes.

Larger lots here mean dogs with room to roam, and that means hair on rugs, paw marks on entry floors, and dander in bedrooms. We slow down on stairs, rugs, and upholstered pieces in pet homes, and we give turnover cleans the same close attention when a farmhouse has to be guest ready between tenants or family visits.
