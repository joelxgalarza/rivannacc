How we work here

An empty-home reset timed to your closing

We clean empty homes top to bottom so nothing gets tracked back through finished rooms. Cabinets, drawers, and closets get wiped inside. Floors and baseboards get a full clean, and kitchens and bathrooms get a deep reset across counters, fixtures, tile, and grout.

Sills, door frames, and vent covers get wiped, since new-build film settles there first. Sellers use the same visit in reverse, with the home left clean for listing photos and walk-throughs.

You do not need to be present. Leave a lockbox code, a garage code, or a door code on the quote form, and tell us about alarms and parking. We schedule around your closing date and your moving truck, with the clean timed so the home is ready when you walk in with boxes.

Closing weeks get busy, so put your date on the form early and tell us when it moves. We work with you on timing.

Rivanna Village specifics

Why new builds need their own kind of clean

Rivanna Village is a newer neighborhood of single-family homes near Glenmore and Keswick along Route 250, where buyers close on just-finished houses. That means fine construction dust on every surface, grit in window tracks, and film inside cabinets and closets that a walk-through wipe never catches.

We wipe closets and cabinets bare, clean floors and baseboards edge to edge, and reset kitchens and bathrooms so the first night in the house feels finished. For homes straight off the build, post-construction cleaning pairs with this visit for heavier dust and debris removal.

After move-in, recurring maid service keeps the new place at baseline on a weekly, biweekly, or monthly plan, and regular visits cost less per clean than one-time visits.
