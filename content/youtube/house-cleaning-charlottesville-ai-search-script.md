### 4.4 How to read and generate a shot

- **AI still:** paste the "Image prompt" into an image model (Midjourney with `--ar 16:9 --style raw`, Higgsfield image, Flux, Nano Banana, or similar). Set 16:9. Attach the set's anchor still as a style or image reference. Pick the best of four. In the editor add a slow push-in (Ken Burns), 100% to 108% scale over the shot.
- **AI motion clip:** generate the "Start-frame prompt" as a still first (same as above), then load that still into an image-to-video tool (Kling, Runway, Higgsfield, Veo, or similar) and paste the "Motion prompt." Set 16:9 and the length listed under "Generate." Image-to-video from an approved still keeps rooms and wardrobe consistent far better than text-to-video.
- **Clip shorter than its slot:** slow it to 80% with optical-flow frame blending, or use the tool's "extend" feature. Never loop a clip.
- **Talking-head placeholder:** filmed live with the host per TH-SET below. No AI.
- **Screen recording:** captured by Joel on a desktop browser at 1920x1080, browser zoom 125%, bookmarks bar hidden, notifications off, cursor highlighted.
- **B-roll placeholder:** real footage Joel films (or owns). No AI.
- **On-screen text card:** built in the editor with the Section 5 templates over the listed background.

**TH-SET (all talking-head shots):**
- Location: a real, clean kitchen that looks like LOC-A (Joel's own, or a client's home with written permission). Host stands behind the island, framed on the left third so text cards fit on the right.
- Camera: 4K, 35mm to 50mm equivalent, eye level, 4 to 5 feet from the host, f/2.8 to f/4 so the background falls soft. Two framings: **Medium** (waist up) for chapter openers, **Medium close-up** (chest up) for quick answers. Alternating between them hides cuts.
- Light: window light from camera left as the key, white bounce card camera right as fill, one practical pendant or under-cabinet light on in the background. Match the warm daylight look of the AI shots.
- Wardrobe: solid charcoal or deep teal shirt. No logos, stripes, or small checks.
- Sound: lav mic or shotgun just out of frame. Turn off the fridge and HVAC. Record 30 seconds of room tone.
- Delivery: read from a teleprompter app, word for word, eyes into the lens.

## 6. AI generation production checklist

### 6.1 Folder and filename convention

```text
RHC-YT01/
  01_script/          this file, teleprompter export
  02_audio/           vo, room tone, music, sfx
  03_aroll/           talking-head takes
  04_anchors/         one approved anchor still per location set
  05_ai-stills/       AI stills and start frames
  06_ai-motion/       AI motion clips
  07_screen/          screen recordings
  08_broll/           filmed B-roll
  09_graphics/        text templates, map, cards, end screen
  10_thumbnail/
  11_exports/
```

Filename pattern: `rhc-yt01_s{shot}_{type}_{slug}_v{version}.{ext}`

| Type code | Meaning | Extension |
|---|---|---|
| `th` | Talking-head take | .mp4 / .mov |
| `vo` | Voiceover line | .wav |
| `anch` | Location anchor still (use `loc-a` to `loc-g` in place of the shot number) | .png |
| `aist` | AI still (final, used directly) | .png |
| `aisf` | AI start frame (feeds a motion clip) | .png |
| `aimo` | AI motion clip | .mp4 |
| `scr` | Screen recording | .mp4 |
| `broll` | Filmed B-roll | .mp4 |
| `map` | Map graphic | .png |
| `thumb` | Thumbnail | .png / .jpg |

Examples: `rhc-yt01_s20_aimo_ceiling-fan_v2.mp4`, `rhc-yt01_loc-a_anch_craftsman-kitchen_v1.png`, `rhc-yt01_s02_th_short-answer_v3.mp4`, `rhc-yt01_s25_vo_older-homes_v1.wav`.

### 6.2 Ordered production steps

1. **Pre-flight decisions (before anything is generated).**
   - Confirm insurance is active. If not, apply the `[VERIFY INSURED]` alternates in Sections 3 and 5.3 and edit the description.
   - Fill `[HOST NAME]`.
   - Leave the optional price line cut (default) unless site policy changes.
   - Pick the talking-head location (TH-SET) and confirm written permission if it is a client's home.

2. **Record audio and talking heads first. They set the timing for everything else.**
   - One session, same room, same mic. Record the 14 ON CAMERA lines on camera (TH-SET), then the 56 VO lines off camera. Two clean takes of each.
   - Record 30 seconds of room tone.
   - Name files by shot number (`rhc-yt01_s05_th_entity_v1.mp4`, `rhc-yt01_s06_vo_cville-albemarle_v1.wav`).

3. **Generate the seven location anchor stills** (tool class: image model with reference support, such as Midjourney, Higgsfield image, Flux, or Nano Banana).
   - For each set (LOC-A to LOC-G in Section 4.2), prompt: "Photorealistic photograph, 16:9. Wide establishing shot of [paste set description]. No people. 24mm lens, standing eye level, level horizon." plus the style line and still negative from Section 4.3.
   - Generate 4 to 8 options, pick one per set, save to `04_anchors/`.
   - Optional: also generate one cleaner reference image (rear three-quarter view, full body, wardrobe per style bible) to use as a character reference in tools that support it.

4. **Generate AI stills and start frames, shot by shot** (image model, 16:9, anchor attached as style or image reference).
   - 17 AI stills (`aist`): shots 03, 08, 11, 12, 19, 31, 38, 44, 47, 58, 59, 60, 62, 63, 64, 65, 70.
   - 30 start frames (`aisf`) for the motion clips: shots 01, 04, 07, 10, 13, 14, 20, 21, 22, 23, 24, 25, 26, 27, 29, 30, 32, 34, 37, 39, 40, 41, 42, 45, 48, 50, 55, 56, 68, 69. Plus shot 36 only if the filmed B-roll is not possible.
   - Check every image at 100% zoom before moving on: five fingers per hand, no letters or logos anywhere (bottles, appliances, clothing, keypads), no visible cleaner faces, no badges, nothing that looks like a mansion.

5. **Animate the 30 motion clips** (tool class: image-to-video, such as Kling, Runway, Higgsfield, or Veo).
   - Load the approved start frame, paste the shot's motion prompt and motion negative, set 16:9, set the length listed under "Generate."

9. **Assemble the edit** (CapCut or Premiere; steps in 6.4).

10. **QA pass** (6.5), then export.

11. **Upload and publish settings:** paste the title, description, and tags from Section 2. Upload the thumbnail. Add the transcript (6.6) under Subtitles with Auto-sync. ### 6.4 Assembly

**CapCut (desktop):**
1. New project, 16:9, 1920x1080 (or 3840x2160), 30 fps.
2. Main track: drop the talking-head takes and VO lines in shot order. Talking-head shots use their own camera or lav audio. VO shots use the VO wav files. Leave a 1-second gap at each chapter change (0:33, 1:07, 2:38, 4:23, 5:39, 7:17, 8:18, 9:10, 10:11). Total should land within a second or two of 11:05.
3. Picture: place each AI clip, still, screen recording, and B-roll over its VO line by shot number. Trim to the shot length. For stills, add a Keyframe scale from 100% to 108% (or the value in the shot).
4. Text: add the Section 5 cards on overlay tracks using the saved templates. Sync each line's entrance to the spoken word.
5. Music on its own track at about -22 dB under VO. Use CapCut's ducking if your version has it, otherwise keyframe the music down 6 dB under talking heads. SFX on a third audio track at about -18 dB.
6. Color: apply one light warm grade to all AI clips, then match the talking heads to them (slightly warm white balance, gentle contrast). Whites should stay white.
7. Do not burn in captions. YouTube captions come from the transcript upload.
8. Export: H.264, 1080p at 16 Mbps or 4K at 45 Mbps, 30 fps, AAC 320 kbps. Target loudness about -14 LUFS integrated.

**Premiere Pro:**
1. Sequence: 1920x1080 or 3840x2160, 29.97 or 30 fps.
2. V1 talking heads and visuals, V2 to V3 graphics (`.mogrt` templates from Essential Graphics), A1 talking-head and VO dialogue, A2 music, A3 SFX.
3. Essential Sound: tag A1 as Dialogue, A2 as Music with Auto-Duck on.
4. Lumetri: one adjustment layer for the overall warm look, plus per-clip matching on the talking heads.
5. Stills: Effect Controls > Scale keyframes 100 to 108.
6. Export: H.264, Match Source High Bitrate, Loudness Normalization on (ITU BS.1770-3, -14 LUFS, true peak -1 dBTP).
